# Memory: features/tax/reporting-and-analytics-v2
Updated: 4m ago

The Sales Tax module supports comprehensive reporting for CRA GST/HST and provincial sales tax (MB PST, QST). It features a 'TaxReportPreview' component that generates detailed summaries and transaction-level reports with PDF export (via jsPDF). Users can filter reports by category (GST vs PST), date range (month, quarter, year), and account type. It includes a 'Compare With' feature to analyze performance against previous periods or years, maintaining alignment with QuickBooks/Zoho Books reporting standards.

Both the Reports tab and Tax Summary tab are now organized into CRA GST/HST and Provincial (PST/QST) category tabs for Canadian organizations. Each category displays:
- Tax Collected (Sales)
- Tax Paid (ITCs)
- Net Payable
- Refund/Owing indicator (showing whether the organization owes or is due a refund)

The Refund/Owing line clearly indicates the tax position with visual color coding (green for refund, red for owing).
