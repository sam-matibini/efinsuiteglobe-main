# Memory: features/tax/reporting-and-analytics-v3
Updated: now

The Sales Tax module provides real-time 'Current Tax Balances' derived from General Ledger accounts and comprehensive reporting for CRA GST/HST and provincial taxes (PST/QST). The 'TaxReportPreview' component enables users to preview and download detailed reports (Summary, Detailed Transactions, or Account Breakdown) with a 'Refund / Owing' line indicator. It includes a 'Compare With' feature for period-over-period or year-over-year analysis, similar to QuickBooks/Zoho Books. Users can filter summaries and reports by tax category, date range, account type, and specific tax codes.
